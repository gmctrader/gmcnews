Three angles, one score

Nothing here calls itself a signal alone.

Structure, macro bias, and news timing each have to agree before a setup earns a grade — every point on the 0–100 confluence score comes with a plain-language reason.

ICT / SMC structure

Swing points, break-of-structure & change-of-character, fair value gaps, and liquidity sweeps — the "where" layer, read fresh off live candles every scan.

DXY macro bias

The dollar's own structure, mapped onto every pair through a correlation table — agreement earns a bonus, conflict costs points or vetoes the trade outright.

News timing

Blackout windows around FOMC/NFP/CPI/PPI shave or veto scores automatically — no fresh setup gets highlighted seconds before a number that can invalidate it.

Precision, not guesses

Every level is derived, never invented.

The stop sits beyond the actual structural point the trade depends on — the swept liquidity level or the last swing — plus an ATR-sized buffer so ordinary noise doesn't clip it. Targets stage out in real R-multiples, snapped to the nearest untouched opposing swing when one sits closer than the pure multiple would land.

You get the exact price, the pip distance, and the resulting reward-to-risk on every target — formatted to the right decimal precision for that instrument, whether it's a JPY pair, gold, or BTC.

How a signal gets built

From raw candles to a tradeable plan.

01

Read structure on two timeframes

HTF and MTF swings are fractal-detected, then walked chronologically to label each break as continuation (BOS) or reversal (CHoCH).

02

Cross-check the dollar

DXY's own bias is mapped onto the symbol via its correlation family — direct or inverse — and compared against what structure already found.

03

Check the news clock

Blackout windows around high-impact releases dock or veto the score; the post-release window gets a small bonus once the dust settles.

04

Compute entry, stop & targets

ATR sizes the noise buffer beyond the structural invalidation point; targets stage out in R-multiples, snapped to real liquidity where it's closer.

05

Send it, if you want

One tap pushes the full plan — entry, stop, targets, reasoning — to Telegram, Discord, or your own webhook, formatted as a normal trading signal.
